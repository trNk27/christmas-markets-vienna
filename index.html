    About this tracker

    A planning aid for Vienna's Christmas markets: pick when you're going and every status, the map and the crowd estimate follow that moment. All times are Vienna time.

    Crowds: there is no live crowd data yet. The busyness shown is a typical estimate based on the market's popularity, the hour and the day — not a measurement.
